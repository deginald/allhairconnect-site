Built by people who do this work

Real students, real pros, real clients

All Hair Connect was built around an actual barbering program and the students, licensed pros, and clients moving through it every day, not a hypothetical one.

Built for every stage

Whoever you are in the beauty industry, there's a toolkit for you

All Hair Connect isn't three separate apps stitched together. It's one platform, with the right tools shown to the right person.

Students

School Toolkit

Everything a beauty industry student needs to track progress and build real skill.

  • Pace: track your hours and graduation date as you go
  • Refresher: step-by-step technique walkthroughs for cuts, color, bleach, perms, and relaxers
  • Ledger: a self-rated skill graph you own, shareable in one link
Licensed & Apprentice Pros

Pro Toolkit

Sign up free with a real account, run your book, and keep clients coming back.

  • Consult: product & treatment recommendations by texture, condition, and scalp
  • Booking, Loyalty & Earnings: appointments, reward tiers, and income tracking
  • Public profile: get found on Find a Pro, filterable by Barber or Cosmetologist
Clients

Hair Passport

A portable record of your hair (texture, formulas, and visit history) that belongs to you, not any one shop.

  • Photos and notes from every visit, in one place
  • Search Find a Pro by city, state, Barber, or Cosmetologist
  • Book appointments, read real reviews, and track rewards with your pro
See it in action

The real screens, doing the real work

These aren't mockups. This is what students, pros, and clients actually see today.

For students · Pace

Always know your graduation date

Log your total required hours and what you've earned so far, and Pace works out your projected graduation date and whether you're on pace, updated automatically as you go.

For students · Refresher

A step-by-step refresher in your pocket

Guided walkthroughs for cuts, color, bleach, perms, and relaxers, broken into consultation, prep, technique, and safety-check steps, so you always know what's next.

Ledger

A skill record that's actually yours

Self-rate every technique as you practice it, and watch your skill graph build over time by category. It's yours to keep and share in one link, no matter where you end up working.

Find a Pro

Search, filter, and book: no account needed

Clients search by city or state and filter straight to Barber or Cosmetologist, then check availability and book directly. No download, no account required to start.

Booking

Real appointments, set by real availability

Pros set their services, pricing, and hours once. Clients pick a service, see real open times, and book, with the total price and duration calculated for them.

Ratings & reviews

Real reviews, from verified visits

Professionalism, skill, shop environment, and kid-friendliness, rated by clients after a real, logged visit, not anonymous internet reviews.

How it works

Three steps to get connected

Create your account

Pros and students sign up free with an email and password. Clients get a simple code (no app-store download required) that becomes their portable hair history.

Connect with your pro or school

Students track hours and refreshers. Pros log visits, consults, and appointments against a real client record.

Grow, together

A stronger record for clients, a stronger skill history for pros, and a stronger pipeline for schools.

Looking for a beauty professional, not building one?

Search by city or state, filter by Barber or Cosmetologist, check availability, and book directly. No account needed to start.
